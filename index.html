ABOUT THE FOUNDATION

Turning collective action into social impact.

InAmigos Foundation was founded on September 23, 2020, by Mr. Govind Shukla (Founder & CEO). The organization is a Section 8 registered non-profit based in Chhattisgarh and works across India.

The foundation works through a network of professionals, volunteers and partners to address social and community needs. Its initiatives span food and clothing support, education, women empowerment, animal welfare, environmental conservation and employability.

Section 8 80G & 12A CSR-1 NITI Aayog ISO 9001:2015*
